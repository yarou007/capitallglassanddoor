A practical scope for a working property

Office entry glass, retail sidelites, restaurant windows and shared commercial entrances each bring different access needs. Capital Glass and Door Services handles commercial glass repair with storefront glass as the main focus.

For a property in Bethesda, MD or Silver Spring, MD, tell us the building name or unit number alongside the street address. The exact location lets us discuss current availability without guessing from a neighborhood alone.

Who we help and what to share

Retail stores need to separate damage from customer traffic. Restaurants need to identify delivery and dining-room access. Offices and medical offices need to coordinate visitors. Property managers need an approved scope and a site contact. These are typical needs, not claims about completed projects.

Share the affected opening, any building rules, trading hours and who can authorize work. Photos should show the full pane and frame from a safe distance. A visible mark or existing specification can help the discussion, but final measurements and material selection need to be checked.

Temporary protection, materials and costs

When a damaged opening cannot be reglazed immediately, discuss board-up. Glass size, thickness, tint, insulated construction, frame condition and access affect the replacement scope and price. Ask how temporary work, final installation and disposal are itemized.

For broken street-facing display or entrance glazing, start with the storefront glass repair guide. Mention a slamming or dragging door if commercial hardware work may be needed too.

Questions before you call

Can a property manager start with photos?

Yes. WhatsApp the damage, address and ZIP code, unit number, business or home type, urgency and approval contact. Photos begin the discussion; they do not establish final measurements.

Do you publish a fixed commercial glass price?

No. Dimensions, glass specification, access, temporary protection and frame condition affect the price. Ask for the scope to be explained for your opening.

Can you work around access restrictions?

Share building access and operating restrictions when you call. The work plan and timing need to be agreed for the particular site.
