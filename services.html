Storefront specialists with supporting services

Capital Glass and Door focuses on storefront glass and emergency board-up for Washington, DC, Northern Virginia and Maryland businesses. Door repair, hardware and locksmith services support the same entrance when more than the pane is damaged.

Tell us what happened, whether glass is loose or missing, and whether the door still closes. For a quote discussion, WhatsApp safe photos, your address and ZIP code, business or home type, and urgency. Call about an active hazard.

Who we help

Retail stores need customer access separated from damage. Restaurants need delivery routes considered. Offices and medical offices need visitor access coordinated. Property managers need a unit number, authorization contact and a clear scope. Share those details so the work can be discussed for your site.

What affects scope, timing and price

Glass size and specification, frame damage, hardware compatibility, temporary board-up, disposal and site access affect the work. Availability and material lead times vary. Ask how temporary protection and permanent repairs are itemized.

Questions before you call

Which service should I choose for broken storefront glass?

Start with storefront glass repair and replacement. If the opening is exposed or the glass is an active hazard, call and mention whether temporary board-up is needed.

Can I discuss glass, locks and door hardware together?

Yes. Explain all visible damage to the entrance so the proposed scope can address the relevant glass, door and lock work.

How do I confirm service availability?

Call with the full address and ZIP code. Tell us which entrance is damaged and how urgent the problem is so we can discuss availability.
