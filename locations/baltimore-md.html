Questions before you call

Do you have a page for Downtown Baltimore, MD?

Yes. The Downtown Baltimore page covers details to share for office, retail and shared-building entrances. Send the full address and current damage photos when contacting us.

What should I report after a Baltimore, MD storefront break-in?

Call about active hazards or an unsecured opening. Describe damaged glass, framing, doors and locks. Keep people away from broken glass and do not approach an unsafe opening for photographs.

Does emergency glass repair mean immediate permanent replacement?

No. The opening may need temporary board-up first. The final panel depends on measurements, glass type, frame condition and material availability; ask for current timing directly.
