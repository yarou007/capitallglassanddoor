Questions before you call

What details do you need to check service availability?

Call with the full property address, ZIP code, affected entrance and urgency. Mention broken glass or an opening that cannot be secured.

What should I send for a storefront glass quote?

Send a safe photo of the damage and whole opening, your address and ZIP code, whether it is a business or home, and how urgent the repair is.

Can I discuss glass and door hardware in one request?

Yes. Mention glass damage and any dragging, slamming, latching, panic bar or lock problem together so the repair needs can be discussed.
