Board-up or storefront replacement in Alexandria, VA?

Board-up covers an exposed opening. Glass replacement restores the glazing. Many incidents need these as two separate stages.

What temporary board-up addresses

A covered opening can provide temporary protection while damaged glazing is assessed and replacement is planned. The method depends on the opening and surrounding structure. Ask what the proposed board-up includes and what remains to be addressed.

What permanent replacement involves

The pane needs a confirmed size, construction, thickness and compatible frame. Door glass may also need specific holes or cutouts. Existing frame or hardware damage can add work beyond replacing the glass itself.

Why the work may be staged

Correct material may not be immediately available, and safe measurement or frame repair may be needed first. Discuss temporary protection and replacement scheduling as separate decisions. Avoid an assumed completion date before the material is confirmed.

Compare the full scope, not only one figure

Ask whether the estimate includes attendance, removal, temporary materials, fabrication, installation and a return visit where needed. Send photos, your address and opening details for an initial discussion; inspection may still be required.

Questions before you call

Is a board-up a permanent repair?

Board-up is temporary protection. Ask for a replacement plan and any site-specific limitations.

Can you replace the glass without boarding up?

That depends on safety, the opening, access and suitable glass availability. Describe the damage and ask which approach is practical.
