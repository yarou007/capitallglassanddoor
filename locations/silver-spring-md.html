Separate glass damage from hardware failure

Describe the whole opening

A storefront door can stop closing because of a closer, pivot or latch problem even when its glass appears intact. If the door and glass were damaged together, describe both. A photograph of the whole door gives context that a tight close-up of a crack cannot provide.

Agree on the next step

For a broken display opening, ask whether temporary board-up should come before measured replacement. The permanent panel may require fabrication, and frame repairs can change the scope. Confirm current availability, replacement timing and any separate temporary-work charges during the estimate discussion.
