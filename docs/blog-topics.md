# Eight additional commercial-intent blog topics

These extend the existing article library rather than publishing duplicate guides.

1. **A delivery cart hit the storefront door: what should a business manager report?** Cover impact symptoms, frame and glass photos, safe access and a call CTA.
2. **Insulated storefront glass is fogging between the panes: repair or replacement?** Explain the need to identify the unit and condition before discussing options; WhatsApp photo CTA.
3. **Two visits for one storefront repair: what temporary secure-up and final installation each include.** Help buyers compare written scopes without promised turnaround times.
4. **Restaurant entry door slamming during service: information to share before a hardware visit.** Focus on customer access, symptoms and operating-window constraints without DIY adjustment instructions.
5. **Panic bar not latching: a manager’s reporting checklist before commercial door repair.** Discuss exit concerns and professional assessment; have jurisdiction-specific legal references reviewed before publication.
6. **Medical office glass door damage: planning access without sharing patient information.** Focus on clear photos, authorized contacts and repair access while avoiding compliance claims.
7. **Replacing storefront glass in a leased space: questions for the tenant and property manager.** Clarify approval and access questions without interpreting a lease or assigning legal responsibility.
8. **Door glass, sidelite or transom: how to describe the damaged storefront opening.** Use a labeled native SVG and WhatsApp photo CTA; no invitation to approach unstable glass.

Write each around a distinct customer decision. Add local context only where useful, link to the matching service page, and finish with Call and WhatsApp. Validate technical and jurisdiction-specific facts against current primary sources before publication.
