# Capital Glass and Door: brand and service search strategy

Updated November 13, 2026. This is an implementation record and marketing plan, not a measurement of live rankings.

## Positioning

Use the owner's stated name, **Capital Glass and Door**, consistently. The business repairs storefront glass and commercial entrances in its documented Washington, DC, Northern Virginia and Maryland service areas. The employment website Glassdoor serves a different search intent. The useful opportunity is to distinguish this repair business for its own name and for local repair requests, rather than attract employment or salary searches.

Natural service wording belongs in headings, titles, descriptions, useful explanations and relevant links. Keyword density targets, hidden text, repetitive city lists and competitor-name stuffing would weaken the customer experience. No reference to the employment website was added to public pages.

## Search intent and landing pages

| Search theme | Intended destination | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital Glass and Door; Capital Glass & Door | Homepage, About, Contact | Identify the business, coverage and its single phone number. |
| Storefront glass repair / storefront glass replacement | Existing storefront glass service page | Explain damaged panes, inspection, specifications and replacement scope. |
| Commercial glass door repair | Existing emergency door repair page | Explain door movement, hardware faults and glass damage; route to the appropriate service. |
| Door closer / panic bar repair | Existing commercial hardware page | Address specific entrance hardware symptoms. |
| Emergency board-up | Existing board-up page | Explain temporary protection separately from permanent replacement. |
| Storefront glass repair + a confirmed city | Existing relevant location page | Provide genuine local access information and links to service details. |

These are relevance targets, not measured keyword-volume or ranking claims. Existing URLs remain unchanged. A second near-duplicate “glass door” landing page was not created; the current commercial door page owns that intent.

## Implemented

- Replaced the previous expanded label “Capital Glass and Door Services” with the owner's stated name in all 53 public HTML pages, including headers, footers, metadata and business JSON-LD.
- Added the ordinary ampersand spelling “Capital Glass & Door” as `alternateName` on the business entity. This is a spelling variant, not a claim of another company or affiliation.
- Added homepage `WebSite` structured data with the brand name, alternate spelling, canonical URL and publisher linked to the existing business entity. Linked the homepage `WebPage` to that website entity. Schema communicates identity; it does not guarantee Google's displayed site name or search position.
- Put the brand first in the homepage title and paired it with storefront glass repair and regional coverage. Added the brand to the homepage introduction and description.
- Made the contact title identify the company and 202-643-4062.
- Strengthened the About heading and description of storefront glass, commercial glass doors and supporting entrance services.
- Refocused the existing emergency door page title/H1 on commercial glass door repair in Alexandria, VA. Added a practical pane-versus-hardware explanation and relevant links to the glass replacement and hardware pages.
- Updated the public text summaries (`llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`) to match the brand and phone format. Removed owner-facing confirmation notes and pending-location routing from those summaries; unconfirmed coverage was not promoted. These files are informational, not an established Google ranking lever.
- Updated the validator's expected business name to match the owner correction. Business-name/telephone consistency checks still run.

No fake credentials, ratings, branch addresses, operating hours, response promises or social profile URLs were introduced. All telephone links continue to use the same 202-643-4062 destination. Earlier mobile conversion improvements remain in place.

## Next measurable marketing work

After deployment, keep the same real business name, website URL and phone number on an eligible Google Business Profile and legitimate business listings. Use only actual owned profiles and confirmed business details. No external listing was created or edited during this work. Do not append keyword lists to the real business name.

Use Search Console to compare branded and nonbranded mobile queries, landing pages, impressions, clicks and CTR. Check branded variations separately from generic “glass door repair” queries. Review indexing of the intended service pages and the canonical homepage after deployment; publication and indexing are not automatic consequences of local edits.

Build supporting evidence through original project descriptions and genuine customer reviews. Publish job locations, customer identities or outcomes only when verified and authorized. Avoid fabricated citations, purchased reviews and unverified local pages.

Track phone-link taps separately from answered calls, qualified leads and booked work. Keep 202-643-4062 as the public number; no dynamic phone-number replacement is needed. Ranking above another site and gaining clients cannot be guaranteed by word changes alone.
