Broken storefront glass: what needs attention first

A smashed display window leaves stock exposed; a cracked door panel can interrupt the only customer entrance. Keep people away from damaged glazing and avoid opening a door that moves loose glass. Call about active hazards. Do not touch or climb near damaged glass.

Capital Glass and Door Services handles storefront glass repair and replacement, with emergency board-up and commercial door repair as supporting services. Tell us whether the opening is a display window, door lite, sidelite or overhead transom so we can discuss the next step.

What causes storefront glass damage?

Impact, attempted entry, damaged edges and pressure from a misaligned frame can all be relevant to an assessment. A slamming door or worn pivot may affect the surrounding opening, so replacing the pane alone may leave another issue unresolved. Share what happened, where the crack begins and whether the door still closes.

Photos cannot confirm glass specification, hidden frame damage or final size. Inspection and measurement determine replacement scope.

Tempered vs laminated storefront glass

Tempered glass is heat treated and typically breaks into small pieces. Laminated glass bonds glass layers to an interlayer that can retain fragments after breakage. Neither makes a damaged opening safe to use or guarantees protection from a break-in.

The right replacement depends on the opening, existing system and applicable requirements. Thickness, tint, insulated construction and any safety markings matter. Do not substitute ordinary glass based on appearance alone. See our tempered and laminated glass guide. Guardian Glass technical guidance explains these glass types.

Board-up first, measured replacement next

When glass cannot be replaced during the initial visit, discuss emergency board-up to cover the exposed opening while replacement is arranged. Temporary boarding and permanent glazing are different jobs; ask what each stage includes and how access will be managed.

  1. Describe the hazard, location and access restrictions by phone.
  2. Review temporary protection and the condition of the frame.
  3. Confirm measurements, glass specification and replacement scope.
  4. Agree on installation access and discuss remaining door or lock issues.

For board-up after a break-in, tell us if the lock, frame or commercial glass door was also damaged. Coordinate access with your property manager before work begins.

How long does storefront glass replacement take?

Arrival and completion are separate questions. Availability, travel, opening size, glass type, fabrication and frame repairs affect timing. A temporary board-up may be needed before a custom pane is ready. Call to discuss the current schedule; a photo alone cannot establish an arrival time or installation date.

Ask when measurements can be checked, when the glass can be obtained and how installation will fit your trading hours.

What it costs / what affects price

A quote depends on panel dimensions, number of panes, material specification, accessibility, frame condition, disposal and any temporary board-up. Door closer, pivot, panic bar or lock repairs can add a separate scope. Ask whether the estimate covers temporary protection, final glazing and related hardware.

Retail stores, restaurants, offices and medical offices need usable entrances. Property managers should include the unit number and approval contact.

Questions before you call

Can cracked storefront glass be repaired instead of replaced?

A cracked pane commonly needs replacement. Its material, damage and frame condition must be assessed before a suitable repair plan can be confirmed.

Can you board up before the replacement glass is ready?

Emergency board-up is an available service. Call to discuss the opening, access and current availability; permanent replacement is a separate stage.

What should I send on WhatsApp?

Send a photo taken from a safe distance, your address and ZIP code, whether this is a business or home, and how urgent the damage is. No measurements are needed to start.

Can you address a door closer or panic bar with the glass?

Commercial door hardware is a supporting service. Mention any slamming, dragging or latching problem when you call so the combined scope can be discussed.
