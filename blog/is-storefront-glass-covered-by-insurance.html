Read the specific policy with your insurer

Ask about the cause of loss, glass coverage, exclusions, limits and deductible. For leased premises, clarify the owner and tenant responsibilities with the relevant parties. Do not assume that every broken storefront pane is covered.

Document safely

Photograph the damage only from a safe location and keep repair estimates and invoices. Ask your insurer what records it requires and whether any approval is needed before permanent repairs. Do not put yourself near unstable glass to gather evidence.

Discuss temporary protection separately

An exposed opening may need attention before final glass can be supplied. Explain the condition to your insurer and ask how temporary protection is handled under the policy. The temporary secure-up scope and permanent replacement scope should be clear.

Get a repair discussion started

WhatsApp a safe photo, address or ZIP, business or home and urgency. Tell us if a property manager or adjuster is involved. For an active hazard, call to discuss the opening and current availability.

Questions before you call

Can the repair company guarantee claim approval?

No. Coverage and claim decisions belong to the insurer under the policy terms.

What should I keep for the claim?

Ask your insurer for its requirements. Safe photos, the repair scope, estimates and invoices are useful records to retain.
