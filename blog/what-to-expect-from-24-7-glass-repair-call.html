One number to discuss your next step.

Start with your location and the hazard

Give the full street address, ZIP and best on-site phone number. Explain whether the opening is exposed, glass is loose or a door is affected. Do not approach damaged glazing to answer a question.

Ask what the first visit can cover

Discuss temporary board-up, inspection and measurement separately from permanent replacement. A pane that must be fabricated may need another visit. Ask who must approve work and how the scope will be confirmed.

Confirm availability and pricing before agreeing

Ask for the available attendance window and whether it can change. Explain access constraints and ask what charges apply to attendance, securing the opening, materials and follow-up. Do not assume emergency attendance means same-day replacement.

Use photos to support the call

WhatsApp a photo from a safe distance, your address, business or home and urgency. Add opening or closing time constraints. Send those details directly on WhatsApp to start a quote discussion.

Questions before you call

How do I check availability for an emergency visit?

Call with your full address and ZIP code, describe the damage and ask for an arrival window.

Will all repairs be completed on the first visit?

The scope depends on safety, access, glass specification, frame damage and material availability. Ask what can be completed and what requires follow-up.
