A glass emergency handoff for Washington, DC property managers

A useful handoff connects the damaged opening, an authorized site contact and a clear approval path for temporary and permanent work.

One number to discuss your next step.

Create one concise incident description

Name the property, address and affected entrance. Note whether the premises can be secured, glass is loose or a door is unusable. Keep tenants away from damage and identify the responsible site contact.

Separate access from work approval

Confirm who can provide keys or meet the technician and who can approve the scope. They may be different people. Share building check-in, loading restrictions and working windows privately before attendance is arranged.

Track temporary and permanent stages

Ask what secure-up work is proposed and whether replacement needs measurement, fabrication or frame repair. Keep those stages distinct in your records so the next manager understands what remains open.

Make the follow-up easy to transfer

Keep safe photos, estimates, invoices and contact details in the property file. Record the replacement specification once confirmed and any appointment arrangements. Do not assume the temporary protection itself resolves door or lock damage.

Questions before you call

Can the tenant send the initial photos?

Yes, if taken safely. The person approving work and the on-site access contact should also be identified.

What details help with multiple damaged openings?

Label each affected opening in your description and photos, and explain any separate glass, frame, door or lock symptoms.
