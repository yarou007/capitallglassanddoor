Describe the whole opening

Describe the exact opening: fixed storefront glass, the glass inside a commercial door, a sidelite beside it or a transom above it. If the door rubs, swings unpredictably or no longer latches, mention that separately. Replacement glass alone may not address a damaged frame, closer or pivot.

Questions before you call

Why include the quadrant in my Washington, DC address?

Street names can occur in different quadrants. Include the street number, NW, NE, SW or SE, ZIP and business name so the correct entrance can be identified.

What if glass is above a Georgetown or Capitol Hill shop entrance?

Keep people away from the affected opening and call to describe the height and access. Do not stand under loose or cracked overhead glass to take a photograph.

Can I request door and glass repair for the same entrance?

Yes. Describe both the glass damage and the door problem when you call. Commercial door hardware and locksmith services are available; the repair scope depends on the inspection.
