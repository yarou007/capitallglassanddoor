Describe what the door does

Does it stop before reaching the frame, scrape the threshold, swing too quickly or close without latching? Note when the symptom occurs and whether it began after impact or glass damage. Avoid repeatedly testing a damaged door.

Several parts can produce similar symptoms

A closer, pivot, hinge, latch, strike or frame alignment issue can affect closing. A short description or safe video helps start the discussion, but it does not identify the cause with certainty. An inspection is needed before deciding on an adjustment or part.

Avoid improvised hardware changes

Do not disconnect a closer, wedge a required exit shut or alter panic hardware to make a door stay closed. Ask your site manager to arrange a safe temporary access plan while the condition is assessed.

Send details for commercial door repair

Share the address or ZIP, business type, urgency and photos of the whole door and visible hardware where safe. Mention glass damage and whether the premises can be secured. Call if the door creates an active access or exit concern.

Questions before you call

Is the closer always the cause?

No. Closing and latching symptoms can involve alignment, pivots, hinges, strikes or other hardware. The door needs assessment.

Can glass and hardware be discussed together?

Yes. Mention both when you contact Capital Glass and Door so the opening and door symptoms can be considered together.
