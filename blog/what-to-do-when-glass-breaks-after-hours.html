Separate an active hazard from a scheduled repair

Falling glass, an unsecured entrance or a door that cannot safely be used needs a phone conversation. A stable, nonurgent issue can start with WhatsApp photos. Service hours and attendance timing must be confirmed when you call.

Arrange an authorized person on site

Tell us who has keys and permission to approve work. A security desk, gated loading area or locked interior corridor can affect access. Share instructions privately by phone rather than posting security details in public.

Plan for board-up when replacement is not ready

The immediate goal may be protecting an opening. A permanent pane depends on accurate measurement, glass type, frame condition and material availability. Ask which stages can be addressed at the first visit and what needs another appointment.

Leave a usable handoff for the morning

Keep photos taken safely, the work scope and contact details together for the owner or manager. Note whether a temporary panel is installed and ask what access or follow-up is needed. Do not assume the building is ready to reopen solely because an opening has been covered.
