What affects the price?

A useful quote starts with the opening and the damage. Glass dimensions, thickness, tempered or laminated construction, damaged framing, door hardware, access, removal and temporary board-up can all affect the scope.

Send a safe photo and your address, then ask what is included: securing the opening, measurement, glass supply, installation and disposal. Confirm availability and timing before authorizing work.

Questions before you call

Should I call or send photos first?

Call for active hazards or broken glass right now. For a nonurgent quote, send a safe photo, address or ZIP code, property type and urgency on WhatsApp.

Can board-up come before replacement glass?

Emergency board-up can provide temporary protection for an exposed opening. Discuss the damage, glass measurements, material availability and replacement plan when you call.

Do you repair commercial doors as well as glass?

Capital Glass and Door Services offers commercial door repair and hardware services, including closers, panic bars and pivots, alongside storefront glass and locksmith services.
