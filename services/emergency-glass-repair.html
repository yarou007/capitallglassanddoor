An exposed opening needs a clear plan

A broken display window, cracked door lite or damaged transom can interrupt business access. Capital Glass and Door Services provides emergency glass repair and board-up for Washington, DC, with service-area pages for Arlington, VA and Baltimore, MD. Keep customers and staff clear of damaged glass. Avoid moving a door with loose glazing. Describe whether the opening is exposed to the street, whether there is another usable entrance and who can authorize access.

Temporary protection and permanent replacement

Some damage requires temporary board-up before replacement glass is available. A temporary cover does not restore the original glazing. Ask about the condition of the frame, the material needed and the separate scope for final installation.

For a damaged commercial storefront, the storefront glass repair page explains glass choices, measurements and replacement planning. Mention damage to locks, closers or pivots so those issues can be reviewed too.

What affects timing and cost

Current availability, travel, safe access, glass size and specification, fabrication and frame condition influence the work. There is no fixed arrival or completion time before those details are checked. Ask which costs relate to temporary protection and which relate to replacement.

For a nonurgent quote, send a clear photo from a safe distance, the full address and ZIP code, and your business or home type. Include whether anyone can meet at the property.

Questions before you call

Do you guarantee a particular arrival time?

No fixed arrival time is published. Call with the address and hazard details to discuss current availability and an estimate for your situation.

Should I send photos before calling about falling glass?

Call about an active hazard. Only take photos from a safe distance if you can do so without approaching damaged glass.

Will replacement always happen during the first visit?

That depends on measurements, glass availability and the opening condition. Temporary board-up may be discussed before permanent replacement.
